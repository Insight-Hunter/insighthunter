What the records represent

Each row represents a state-level observation tied to health insurance coverage, marketplace participation, Medicaid, and Medicare measures. The file also includes a United States summary row, which supports comparison between state-level values and the national total.

Example entries begin with Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado.
